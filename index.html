About this app

Just Local Notes helps you quickly write and keep notes in your browser.

Everything stays on this browser profile, so it is private and simple to use.

Notes are not synced to the cloud, and they are only available here on this device.

What this app does

Just Local Notes is a simple note-taking app designed for quick writing and saving right in your browser.

Where your notes are stored

Your notes are saved locally in this browser profile using browser storage. They are not uploaded to any server or shared with anyone.

Important to know

These notes are only available in this browser profile on this device. If you use another browser, another profile, or clear this site data, your notes may not be there anymore.

This is a local-only app, so it is best for personal notes you want to keep on this device.
